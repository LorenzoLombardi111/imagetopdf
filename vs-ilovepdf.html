freeimagetopdf.app vs iLovePDF — Free Image to PDF Comparison

Both freeimagetopdf.app and iLovePDF can turn your images into PDF files, but they take fundamentally different approaches to how your files are handled. This comparison breaks down the differences so you can choose the tool that fits your needs best.

What makes iLovePDF a popular choice?

iLovePDF has earned its reputation as one of the most widely used online PDF tools. It offers a clean, approachable interface with batch conversion support, letting you upload multiple images and combine them into a single PDF quickly. Beyond image-to-PDF conversion, iLovePDF bundles dozens of tools under one roof: PDF merging, splitting, compression, Word conversion, and much more. If you frequently work with PDFs in different ways, having everything in a single platform is genuinely convenient. The service also supports multiple languages and has mobile apps for iOS and Android.

Why does privacy matter when converting images to PDF?

This is where the two tools diverge most sharply. iLovePDF requires you to upload every image to their servers for processing. Your files pass through their infrastructure, are processed remotely, and then returned to you as a download link. While iLovePDF states that files are deleted after a period of time, your images still travel across the internet and sit on third-party servers, however briefly.

freeimagetopdf.app takes the opposite approach: your images never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. There is no upload, no server, and no network request involved in the conversion. For anyone handling sensitive documents, personal photos, medical records, or business materials, this distinction is significant. Your files remain entirely under your control.

Do I need an account or is there a daily limit?

iLovePDF imposes daily task limits on free users. Once you exceed the free tier, you are prompted to subscribe to a premium plan. Some features, like batch processing beyond a certain number of files, are gated behind the paywall. You can create a free account to get slightly higher limits, but the caps remain.

freeimagetopdf.app requires no account, no sign-up, and enforces no daily limits. You can convert as many images as you want, as many times as you want, entirely free. There are no watermarks added to your output, and no premium tier because there is nothing to upsell.

Which tool gives you more control over the PDF output?

For image-to-PDF conversion specifically, freeimagetopdf.app offers a more complete set of layout controls. You can choose from standard page sizes (A4, Letter, Legal, and more), set custom margins, select portrait or landscape orientation, rotate individual images, and adjust output quality with a dedicated slider. These controls let you produce a polished PDF without needing a separate editor.

iLovePDF provides basic page size selection and orientation, but the fine-grained controls for margins, per-image rotation, and quality adjustment are limited in its image-to-PDF tool. For straightforward conversions with default settings, iLovePDF works fine. For precise layout control, freeimagetopdf.app has the edge.

Can I work offline?

Because freeimagetopdf.app runs entirely in your browser with no server dependency, it works offline once the page has been loaded. This is useful on flights, in areas with poor connectivity, or simply when you want a tool that does not depend on a remote service being available. iLovePDF requires an active internet connection for every conversion because files must be uploaded and processed server-side.
